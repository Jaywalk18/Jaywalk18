## 🧑‍🔬 About Me

<picture>
  <img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/JIX9t2j0ZTN9S/giphy.gif" width="260" alt="cat typing">
</picture>

- 🎓 &nbsp;Ph.D. student (**combined Master's–Ph.D. program**) at the [**College of Systems Engineering, NUDT**](https://www.nudt.edu.cn), Changsha, China
- 🔬 &nbsp;Research focus: **self-supervised / contrastive representation learning**, from minimal contrastive components (CORE) to semantic-guided augmentation (SDI)
- 🌌 &nbsp;Extending SSL into **generative modeling** (CMAE: manifold autoencoder + latent diffusion) and **rotation-equivariant 360° perception** (EquiSSL)
- 🌈 &nbsp;Also exploring **infrared–visible multi-modal fusion** for video understanding (TriCM-Net)
- 🛠️ &nbsp;Builder of **AI-powered academic tools**: LaTeX translation, multi-agent paper review, BibTeX verification
- 📖 &nbsp;Running a daily [**Visual SSL Paper Digest**](https://jaywalk18.github.io/visual-ssl-digest/) site
- ✉️ &nbsp;Reach me at **zhoutianjian2000@nudt.edu.cn** — always open to collaboration
