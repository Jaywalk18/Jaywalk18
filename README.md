### 🎓 &nbsp;About Me

🔬 &nbsp;I do research on **self-supervised / contrastive representation learning** and its extension to generative modeling.\
🎓 &nbsp;I'm a Ph.D. student (combined Master's–Ph.D. program) at the **College of Systems Engineering, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT)**, Changsha, China.\
📝 &nbsp;I have published **5 papers** — first author on *ACM TOG (SIGGRAPH Asia 2026)*, *IEEE TNNLS*, and *Pattern Recognition*, co-first author on *ICME 2026*, plus *PRCV 2026*.\
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently exploring gauge equivariance for 360° perception and minimal architectures for contrastive learning.\
🛠️ &nbsp;On the side, I build **AI-powered academic tools** — LaTeX translation, multi-agent paper review, reference verification — to make research workflows smoother.\
📖 &nbsp;I also run a daily [**Visual SSL Paper Digest**](https://jaywalk18.github.io/visual-ssl-digest/) site.\
✉️ &nbsp;Feel free to reach me at **zhoutianjian2000@nudt.edu.cn** for collaboration or just an interesting discussion!
