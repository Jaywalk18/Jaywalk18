## 🎓 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student** (combined Master's–Ph.D. program) at the **College of Systems Engineering, National University of Defense Technology (NUDT)**, Changsha, China.

My research focuses on **self-supervised / contrastive representation learning** and its extension to generative modeling — from minimal contrastive components (CORE), semantic-guided augmentation (SDI), manifold autoencoders for latent diffusion (CMAE), to rotation-equivariant 360° scene understanding (EquiSSL) and infrared–visible fusion (TriCM-Net).

I also enjoy building **AI-powered academic tools** — LaTeX translation, multi-agent paper review, reference verification — to make research workflows smoother.
